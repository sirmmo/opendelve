    OpenDelve

    Dig a dungeon one piece at a time.

    1. Dig from the stairs. Lay each piece against an open doorway (the glowing squares). Doors must meet doors and walls must meet walls. You can't seal the last way on while pieces remain.
    2. Finish rooms. When every door of a room leads somewhere, it gets furnished: a storeroom, guardroom, armoury, crypt or great hall. Rooms finished from depth 9 down also hold treasure.
    3. Delve deep. Depth is the number of pieces between a room and the stairs. Reaching a new depth is worth glory, and doors that join the same style (stone or cave) are worth more.
    4. Finished rooms and quests bring new pieces. When the last piece is laid, the delve is over.
    5. Bring your own map. From the menu, import a map made with One Page Dungeon and dig on from it: doors laid against its old walls break through.
    Mouse · click a glowing square to lay · R or right click for the next way it fits · drag to move · right drag to rotate the view · wheel to zoom
    Touch · tap a spot, tap again to lay · ⟲ ⟳ for the next way it fits · drag, pinch and twist the view
    Keys · Z undo · X discard · C center · Q/E rotate the view · WASD move
