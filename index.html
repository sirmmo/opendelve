    OpenDelve

    Dig a dungeon one piece at a time.

    1. Dig from the stairs. Lay each piece against an open doorway (the glowing squares). Doors must meet doors and walls must meet walls. You can't seal the last way on while pieces remain.
    2. Finish rooms. When every door of a room leads somewhere, it gets furnished: a storeroom, guardroom, armoury, crypt or great hall. Rooms finished from depth 9 down also hold treasure.
    3. Ancient rooms. Some rooms come already in ruins: a lost library (shelves, open at both ends), a shrine (a statue in the corner) and a fallen gallery (broken columns under an arch). Finish them like any room. A finished library and shrine at most 3 steps apart found an Academy (+15, +1 piece). A finished gallery with a finished library and a finished crypt each at most 3 steps from it found an Archaeological Society (+22, +2 pieces), and from then on every treasure is worth 6 more. A step is one door from piece to piece.
    4. Delve deep. Depth is the number of pieces between a room and the stairs. Reaching a new depth is worth glory, and doors that join the same style (stone or cave) are worth more.
    5. Finished rooms and quests bring new pieces. When the last piece is laid, the delve is over.
    6. Sandbox. From the menu, start a sandbox and choose every piece yourself from the tray at the bottom, stone or cave. Pieces never run out; it has its own save, so your delve waits for you.
    7. Bring your own map. From the menu, import a map made with One Page Dungeon and dig on from it: doors laid against its old walls break through.
    Mouse · click a glowing square to lay · R or right click for the next way it fits · drag to move · right drag to rotate the view · wheel to zoom
    Touch · tap a spot, tap again to lay · ⟲ ⟳ for the next way it fits · drag, pinch and twist the view
    Keys · Z undo · X discard · C center · Q/E rotate the view · WASD move
    Sandbox · click or tap a piece in the tray · [ ] previous and next piece · T stone or cave
